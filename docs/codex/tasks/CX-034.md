## Goal and evidence

Implement reusable province gameplay on the CX-033 contested engine. An army
should be able to reveal a frontier, explore a province, discover resources
and sites, decide whether to engage a site guard, and bring the economic
benefit back into the faction turn loop. Spend those gains on construction,
a hero and equipment, then use that army to explore, negotiate or fight
again. Deliver these systems in one strategic mode with one production
loop and checkpoint. Connect the province, settlement, hero/army and encounter
screens to authoritative commands.

Read AGENTS.md, docs/WORK_ALLOCATION.md, DELIB-0008 and CX-033, especially the
Province and settlement reference in docs/CX033_CONTESTED_CAMPAIGN.md.
The reference shows unexplored space, terrain, exploration percentage,
income, resource and local-status fields, and a pre-battle decision panel.
Implement the native contract below. Record authored defaults separately from
observations of Genesis/NH. The private clip is optional for execution.

## Province model and actions

- Extend the existing contested engine through an authored province rules
  option and one playable campaign definition that enables it. Preserve
  existing contested definitions, save fingerprints, round ordering and
  routes, as well as the older native campaign modes. Exercise the new rules
  on a second graph in tests. Keep province identifiers and content out of
  command dispatch and presentation logic.
- Keep per-faction knowledge for each province. Seed home/frontier knowledge
  from authored content; successful movement reveals the destination and
  makes its neighbors discoverable. Unknown provinces show no terrain,
  income, garrison, site or resource details. Frontier markers may show that
  there is a reachable route. Explored information persists after departure;
  clearly distinguish remembered or unknown details from current visibility.
  Use the same knowledge boundary for player presentation and rival
  decisions. Keep omniscient state internal to the model and save validator.
- Add **Explore** for a living field army in its current friendly or neutral
  province, using the same per-army and per-troop movement budget as Travel.
  Travel and Explore compete for the round. Progress is deterministic,
  bounded to 100%, and uses authored increments and discovery thresholds.
  Refuse a completed exploration without spending movement. Transfer and
  formation never restore movement. Reuse the CX-033 command/query boundary.
  Authored terrain and local status are visible context; only display status
  fields that have represented values.
- Discovery reveals an authored resource and/or site. A discovered resource
  begins contributing its authored gold yield while the province is owned by
  the faction controlling it, starting at the next eligible end-of-round
  payout. Define discovery per faction and explicitly handle capture, loss
  and rediscovery so the bonus cannot go to the wrong owner or pay twice.
  Show base and resource income distinctly; keep the existing castle income
  timing and treasury rules.
- A discovered site has persistent status. Inspect it and offer **Fight**, **Withdraw** and where supported **Negotiate** in a pre-battle panel. Fight requires the army at the site and
  available movement, and opens a real encounter through CampaignEncounter.
  A site guard is separate from the province garrison. Withdraw, or abandon
  before resolution, preserves the map state, movement and rewards, subject
  to the existing monotonic attempt-ID rule. Apply a completed result once:
  update the attacking roster and site guards, spend movement, and award
  authored gold once on a win. Preserve site guard casualties and wounds
  after a loss, including across save/resume. A site battle never transfers
  province ownership. Use the existing tactical command and battle UI.
- Rival logic reads the same faction-facing information and uses the same
  legal command API. It may explore or engage sites under authored policy
  weights, with deterministic ordering and the existing action bound. Give
  ruin rewards, resource yields, knowledge state, progress and movement no
  independent UI authority.

## Province economy and settlements

- Add gold and a second strategic resource (crystals) to the enabled mode.
  Per-faction ledgers use named integer balances. Sites, discovery and
  settlement output can yield either resource through one authoritative payout
  path. Prices may use both. Refusal for either insufficient balance is atomic.
  Expose base yield, bonuses and any known resource requirement. Construction
  and discoveries use a declared, consistent payout boundary; existing
  definitions retain their current timing.
- Promote the construction catalogue to province-local settlements. Capital
  districts retain the existing catalogue and recruit chains. Add at least
  one buildable non-capital district with an economic facility and a local
  recruitment facility. Definitions declare eligible settlement/terrain,
  prerequisites, prices, effects and unlocks. Use province-local prerequisite
  checks and garrison capacity. Expose the service target and remaining slot.
  Share the existing faction service budget for buildings, troop and hero
  recruitment, recovery, and equipment purchases. Balance the new content to
  make the loop playable without scripted grants.
- Give provinces an authored starting settlement tier and local stability
  value. In the enabled mode, capture lowers stability, owned turns restore
  it, and income is scaled by configurable integer thresholds. Render the
  real state, recovery expectation and effective payout. Verify loss/recapture
  cannot reset the recovery clock for free. Facilities stay with the province;
  effects and service access follow current control.

## Heroes, equipment and formation

- Implement persistent hero identities and an authored hire catalogue with
  at least two roles. Hire into a friendly settlement, assign to a co-located
  army, and reassign or withdraw by guarded commands. An army can have at
  most one leader and a hero can lead only one army; unassigned heroes stay
  at an owned settlement. Existing garrisons and unled armies still work.
- A hero deploys as a real linked tactical participant using an authored
  definition and existing combat stats. Account for that slot at query and
  deployment validation. Resolve hero wounds and death once through detached
  results, and define what happens to unassigned heroes on capture and
  to an army that loses its leader. No implicit resurrection on transfer.
- Add deterministic XP from resolved victories, configurable thresholds,
  and at least one earned level that changes a supported tactical stat.
  Add a small authored inventory with equipment purchase, equip and
  unequip, slot compatibility and unique ownership. Use ordinary tactical
  numeric stat composition at encounter construction. Display base and
  effective stats. Equipment and leveling must preserve current wounds,
  and temporary tactical state must stay outside the strategic checkpoint.
- Provide a hero/army screen with leader, equipment, troop and garrison
  panes. Allow deterministic tactical deployment ordering from army slots;
  keep existing transfer guards and defense capacity. Exercise assigning,
  ordering and deploying a hero-led army with ordinary tactical commands.

## Encounter decisions

- Give sites and defended neutral destinations a pre-battle decision with
  Fight and Withdraw, plus Negotiate where authored. Preview only known
  information and exact legal quotes. For the initial negotiation rule,
  implement a deterministic authored tribute price and settlement outcome:
  a site grants safe passage or resolves its access without battle rewards;
  an eligible neutral garrison yields control without combat. Make terminal
  decisions explicit and persist them. Pay once, spend movement once, and
  reject stale decisions after position, ownership or funds change. Withdraw
  leaves the army at its origin for a travel encounter and on the province
  for a site encounter. Keep existing rival-faction tactical defense flow.
- Extend rival decisions to use the new information, service and encounter
  APIs with legal, deterministic choices. Exercise at least one rival choice
  using the new systems. Roles, terms, rewards and progression are authored
  native design; no need for original binary constants.
