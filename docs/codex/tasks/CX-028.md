## Player loop

- Add a strategic campaign entry reachable from the main menu. Preserve the
  standalone tactical demo, direct hot-seat scene, and short Ford/Watchpost
  demo as playable entry points. The strategic menu can offer New and Resume
  on a single landing screen.
- Author six provinces in this topology, using stable province IDs:
  Home connects to Ford and Quarry, Ford connects to Watchpost, Quarry
  connects to Mill, and Watchpost and Mill each connect to Keep. Links are
  bidirectional. Home starts friendly and is the starting location. All
  other provinces start hostile. Keep is the objective. Either route can
  reach victory, and the player can backtrack through friendly provinces.
- The map shows position, ownership, travel options, objective, gold, turn
  budget, and a roster with current/max life and visible casualties. A
  friendly adjacent destination moves the army. A hostile adjacent
  destination opens its tactical battle. Invalid, non-adjacent, repeated
  in-flight, and terminal commands leave state unchanged.
- The battle uses fresh tactical objects and instance IDs and deploys only
  living members. Azure remains player-controlled and Crimson automated.
  Preserve the campaign roster's identity, current life, and casualty data.
  Resolve battles through CampaignEncounter.collect and CampaignRoster.apply.
  A valid victory captures the destination and moves the army there. A
  Crimson victory or draw ends the campaign in defeat. Apply a completed
  result at most once. An unfinished, halted, abandoned, or invalid battle
  applies no result, spends no turn, and grants no capture or income.
  Expose a way back to the map from a halted battle without committing it.
- Capturing Keep wins. Exhausting the authored turn budget without capturing
  Keep loses. A victory on the final available turn counts. A complete
  campaign remains inspectable and can start a fresh run. A new run never
  implicitly overwrites an existing checkpoint.

## Economy and turn accounting

- Start with an authored gold balance and turn budget. Each successful
  friendly move, completed encounter, paid recovery, and recruitment uses
  one turn. Opening a battle, inspecting the map, saving, loading, and
  refused commands use zero turns. There is no free income action.
- After a turn commits, credit the authored income of all provinces now
  owned, including a newly captured province. Affordability is checked
  against the pre-action balance. Spend and change roster/state atomically.
- Recovery and recruitment are available at Home. Recovery costs a fixed
  authored gold amount and adds an authored amount of life to each wounded
  survivor up to that member's maximum. It cannot revive casualties or charge
  an army with no wounds to heal. Recruitment costs an authored amount and
  adds a full-life member from an authored recruit definition with a new
  roster ID. Cap the number of living deployed units at four. A casualty
  frees a slot while its persistent record stays visible. Author starting
  and recruit definitions and enough deployment positions for the cap.
- Balance the default map so the player can afford a meaningful paid action
  before the final battle and can finish without using either service.
  Record a short playthrough for each route, including one using both
  services. Exact tuned values are delegated within the native scope.

## Checkpoint and architecture

- Save on the strategic map to one local checkpoint under user://. Resume
  from the campaign entry after leaving the run or restarting the game.
  Store versioned plain values for the strategic state and a stable authored
  content identity. Reconstruct from the current authored definitions. Never
  serialize a node, tactical session, tactical RNG, or a mid-battle state.
- Validate a checkpoint before using it, including version/content identity,
  map position/ownership, turn and gold ranges, roster IDs/types/life, and
  terminal-state consistency. An unreadable, malformed, or incompatible
  checkpoint gives a readable reason and leaves the current run untouched.
  Publish a new checkpoint only after writing a complete replacement so a
  write failure preserves any prior valid slot. Make the save path injectable
  for tests. Tests must use a temporary slot.
- Keep campaign authority headless in core/campaign. Keep disk I/O, display,
  input, and scene transitions in game/. UI asks the model which commands
  are available and invokes the same guarded commands that tests exercise.
  Prefer reusing existing campaign and tactical entry points. Tactical
  compatibility rules, combat mechanics, existing fixture goldens, and
  the Python oracle stay outside this milestone.
- Out of scope: strategic AI, legacy strategy parity, generic mod/schema
  infrastructure, procedural maps, diplomacy, multiple save slots, migrations
  for future checkpoint versions, and save/resume within a battle.
