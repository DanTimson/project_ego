## Presentation requirements

- **Menu.** Build a readable title screen around an illustrated backdrop
  and framed rows with normal, hover and focus states. Candidate pieces are
  Interface:84MainMenu (frame with an open center), Interface:85Eador
  (wide panorama candidate to inspect before cropping), Interface:86MenuStrN
  and Interface:87MenuStrA (menu strips). Retain Project EGO branding,
  build metadata, all six actions and the dialogs. Do not stretch the
  panorama to fill a fixed rectangle or use a screenshot as a background.
- **Strategic map.** Keep the six-province graph and expose its adjacency
  visually. Replace the dominant button-grid look with an illustrated
  terrain surface, distinctive landmark sprites, ownership shields and a
  clearly different current-position ring. MapObj:24Herb01 and 25Herb02
  are red and blue shields. MapObj:40Selection is the ring, and 41PathG
  is a route-marker candidate. Prefer the baked shields initially to
  composing/recoloring Flag:00Main and its masks. Inspect Nature for
  terrain and Site:011Tower, 013Windmill and the verified Castle or OutBld
  sprites for landmarks. Keep navigation and ownership legible without
  relying on color. Show gold with Interface:76Gold; the Resource archive
  contains commodity icons. Do not imply new resource mechanics.
- **Home.** Give recovery and recruitment their own visual area tied to
  Home. Candidate art: Castle:000Background, Castle:001Estate,
  Castle:016RecruitPointe, Castle:017Barracks, and the framed
  Interface:52Dialog01 or Interface:65UnitTypeInfo. Use inspectable
  roster rows with portraits, current/max life, casualty labels and
  costs. Settlement art is visual context for current services.
- **Tactical.** Restore sprites, shadows, portraits, grass variations
  and controls across all battle entry points. Inspect Battlefield:02Panel
  at native resolution (140x768) and use its actual slot boundaries to
  anchor portrait, stat, action and effect groups. Do not simply stretch it
  under the current sidebar. Reflow feedback, events, and campaign controls
  where needed. Inspect Battlefield:13UnitBar, 14Target and 16Selection
  for overlays, Ability:001Life, 002Attack, 004Defence, 008RangedAttack,
  010Ammo, 011Stamina and 012Morale for stats, and Buttons:08ButtEndN
  through 11ButtEndI for Pass states. Keep actual status and action meanings
  explicit, including Tangled Bowstring and Stamina Drain. Inspect Units,
  Unit_icons and matching shadow sheets before assigning roles. Select
  campaign artwork by explicit stable presentation roles or canonical IDs,
  with enemy coverage and retries. Avoid display-name heuristics and
  parsing attempt IDs. Confirm selected sprites, highlights and overlays
  agree with the existing cell transform and hit testing.

Assets above are verified IDs and visual candidates, not mandatory bindings.
Inspect crops, alpha/color key, native dimensions, and visual fit before
using them. Choose other indexed art when it fits better. Avoid implementing
additional gameplay to fill unused original screen slots.
