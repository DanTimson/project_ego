## Resolved during review

- Both positions converged on three explicit coherent profiles and rejected
  permanent independent compatibility flags.
- R8 is separated into Genesis evidence, deliberate native reuse and an
  unresolved NH assignment.
- Stamina-driven effective-speed reduction was recognized as an ordinary R8
  divergence case; the NH observation is not limited to capacity restoration.
- NH charge, NH attack cost and NH RNG topology remain explicitly unresolved and
  must fail loudly when an unassigned NH rule is requested.
- Project EGO's Genesis continuation state was separated from the still-open
  historical question of whether Genesis persisted its live CRT state.
- Architecture, governance and human decision boundaries were recorded without
  requesting another engine or binary review.
